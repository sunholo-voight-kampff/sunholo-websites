The Studio

Crafting botanical narratives in the heart of Edinburgh.

Meet the Maker

Founded on a love for Edinburgh's unique light and landscape, Thistle & Bloom is led by a passion for sustainable sourcing and artisanal techniques. We believe flowers are more than decoration—they are an experience.

Our Philosophy

Sustainable Sourcing

We prioritize locally grown Scottish blooms and eco-friendly mechanics to ensure our impact is as beautiful as our arrangements.

Bespoke Artistry

No two arrangements are ever the same; every design is a unique response to our clients and the season's best offerings.

Timeless Elegance

We lean into classic aesthetics that never feel dated, blending historic charm with contemporary floral artistry.
