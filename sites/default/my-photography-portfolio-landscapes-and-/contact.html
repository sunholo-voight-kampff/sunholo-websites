Questions

Common Enquiries

How far in advance should I book for a wedding?

Popular dates in spring and summer fill 12–18 months ahead. If you have a fixed date, enquire as soon as possible. Off-peak dates (autumn, winter) are often available with less notice.

Do you travel for landscape commissions?

Yes — I travel throughout the British Isles and Europe. Travel costs are discussed transparently during the booking process. I'm particularly happy to head somewhere remote.

What's included in wedding packages?

All wedding bookings include a pre-wedding consultation, full-day coverage, a private online gallery of edited high-resolution images, and a handcrafted USB keepsake. Albums are available as an add-on.

Can I commission a specific landscape location?

Absolutely. If you have a location in mind — a place that means something to you — I'd love to discuss it. Commission shoots can be arranged for personal gift prints, interior art, or property photography.
