SAFETY
FIRST

We are committed to the highest standards of safeguarding for all our participants and staff.

Our Commitment

Regular Training

Every member of our team undergoes rigorous safeguarding training to identify and respond to any concerns effectively.

DBS Checks

Enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service checks are mandatory for all staff and volunteers working with children and vulnerable adults.

Clear Reporting

We maintain transparent and accessible reporting procedures, ensuring that any issues are addressed with urgency and professional care.

Ensuring a safe environment is not just a policy—it is the foundation of everything we do. We strive to provide a space where every rider feels secure, respected, and empowered.
